# The DCR Delusion: Measuring the Privacy Risk of Synthetic Data

*Skimmed*

## Quick Notes
- They compared MIA with simpler proxy metrics like DCR for evaluating privacy leakage in synthetic data generated by classic generators, CTGAN and diffusion models
- Concluded that not only simple distance-based metrics like DCR are not a good estimator of privacy leakage but also the privacy score obtained using these metrics has no corelation with privacy scores assigned by MIA
- Implemented the basic shadow-based MIA for privacy evaluation in black-box and white-box settings
