# AgentHarm: A Benchmark for Measuring Harmfulness of LLM Agents

*Skimmed*

## Quick Notes:
- Built a benchmark to evaluate the agentic capabilities to comply with harmful requests. As agents use external tools via tool calling and can perform multi-step tasks are riskier to be misused
- Their benchmark covers a wide range of harmful requests and is easy to use as it integrated to inspect-ai package
- They realized that many models easily comply with harmful requests without jailbreaking attempts and universal jailbreaking prompts can be easily adapted to the agentic setup to increase the models' compliance
